## Graph truncated

When the normalizer discarded something for the selected session (or, in a view that groups sessions, for the sessions that view counts added up: the ones All shows, or those of the team), a "Graph truncated: N events ignored, M nodes dropped" notice appears under the top bar, followed by ", at least K updates not read" when the source changed rows that the reader was no longer following (OpenCode, see [OpenCode sessions](runtimes.md#opencode-sessions); K is a lower bound and these are not counted as ignored events). **Details** lists the counters by reason under two headings: **Losses** (earlier events not replayed, when there are some, then events ignored, malformed inputs, nodes dropped and updates not read) and **Adjustments (not losses)** (fields shortened or clamped, duplicate events skipped: nothing was left out of the graph for them). Duplicates skipped and fields shortened alone do not trigger it. The cross is "Dismiss truncation warning". A dismissal is kept for the browser tab (sessionStorage), per view (All, a team or one session), with the total it was dismissed at: reloading the page in the same tab does not show it again, a new tab does. It comes back when more is lost in that view; since the relay's figure is a total since the relay started, a page loaded later on a relay that kept receiving events of a long session can show it again. With the relay, it also comes back when the total of the view is lower than the one it was dismissed at: the relay was restarted (its figure starts again from 0) or the graph was reloaded, so this is a new loss, not part of the one dismissed. In the VS Code extension without the relay, a lower total does not bring it back: the extension lowers its figure while the page is open (a session's history read again reaches the page, for instance a Claude Code session that resumes after 10 minutes of silence), and that lower figure is part of the loss already dismissed. So there the notice comes back only when the total of the view goes above the one it was dismissed at. A lower total, even for a moment (a view rebuilt: a new session, a session gone inactive, a project filter), never brings it back and never changes the total stored for the dismissal. Honest limit: a new loss smaller than the gap between the dismissed total and the lowest total seen since is not signalled by the notice (the ⚠ on the Sessions button and the line in the Sessions panel header still show the loss). When the notice was showing (the total went above) and the total falls back to the dismissed one or below without a gesture, the notice goes, nothing is announced, and a focus that was inside it moves to the Sessions button of the top bar. Dismissing it in All also covers each session All counted, at that session's own total, so opening one of them right after does not show it again. When the browser refuses the storage, a dismissal lasts until the page is reloaded. While a loss is known, a short line stays in the header of the Sessions panel, under the agent counts, even after a dismissal: "⚠ at least N events not replayed" and/or "N events ignored, M nodes dropped", with the numbers of the notice for the same view, in the language of the interface. Its **Show notice** button reopens the notice with **Details** expanded and puts the focus on **Details** (only then: when the notice comes back on its own later, after full screen for instance, the focus stays where it is). While the notice is closed and a loss is known, the Sessions button of the top bar carries ⚠ too, so the loss stays visible with the panel closed; screen readers hear it with the button name ("graph incomplete, losses listed in the Sessions panel"), and it goes away while the notice shows. Adjustments alone draw neither the notice, nor this line, nor the mark. While it shows, the graph is partial: counts, "none observed" and "no loop" verdicts only cover what was kept. The same notice also covers the events the relay could no longer replay when the graph was first loaded (its buffer evicted them or its replay cap left them out): "Graph incomplete: at least N earlier events were not replayed". Only the first connection of a graph counts: the graph was empty then, so none of those events had been received, and N is a lower bound. The relay's figure is a total since the relay started, not what a given page lacks; a later reconnection that keeps the graph already received most of those events live, so its new figure is ignored and the first one stays (the hole it described is still there, also while the link is down). What a reconnection adds is what the cut link itself lost: the page tells the relay the last event it received, and the relay counts, per session, the events after it that it can no longer give back (evicted from its buffer while the link was down, or left out by the replay cap); they are added to the count of their session. The same goes while the page stays connected: a history the relay reads meanwhile (a session it starts following, with the sub-agent and teammate files already there) is sent once it is read, after the buffer has applied its cap, so the events that cap let go of never reach the page; the relay sends their count per session right after that history, and the page adds it to the count of their session. It is a lower bound too, and only when the relay kept running: a relay restarted while the link was down numbers its events anew, so nothing is added for that cut (see [relay-sources.md](../relay-sources.md)). It starts again after a scope change, which reloads the view. For one session it is that session's own count; for All it is the count of the sessions All shows (the ones it leaves out while **Hide inactive** is on, or that a project filter leaves out, are not counted), and the events of sessions the relay no longer names are added only when All shows every session; for a team it is the count of the sessions of that team only. A session the relay does not name is never reported as incomplete on that ground, and events a restarted relay never saw cannot be counted. In the VS Code extension (no relay), the same notice gives the extension's own figure: a panel that opens, or reopens, is re-sent only the agents of the active Claude Code sessions (the other runtimes only announce their sessions again), so the events the extension emitted before that page was ready (while the panel was closed or loading, and those an earlier page received) are missing; the extension counts them per session, minus what it re-sends, and sends that figure after the page's reset. A session whose history is read again (Claude Code rediscovers a transcript that grows again after 10 minutes of silence, with the team it leads; the JSONL event log connected again) is not counted twice, and the open page's figure goes down by what that history re-sends to it. OpenCode sessions are not counted: their re-reads cannot be told from new events. It is a lower bound too, and a page reloaded inside the same panel gets no figure (see [relay-sources.md](../relay-sources.md)). The count and the loop verdicts above stay "at least" / "incomplete" while it applies. In the agent card, the figure is the count of that agent's own session, whatever the view: never the total of All or of a team, never the events of sessions the relay no longer names, and nothing for a session the relay does not name. There, the tool count and the tool errors read "at least N", "0 tool errors" becomes "no tool error observed (incomplete)", and in the delegation report the list is named "at least N sub-agents" for screen readers, files read say "at least N files", "none confirmed (incomplete)" or "none observed (incomplete)", and the result says "No return observed (incomplete)" (or "Finished, no return observed (incomplete)" for a sub-agent seen to finish) instead of a final answer; a line gives the missing count. In the Files & repeats panel, the figure is the one of the notice (the selection: one session, All, or the sessions of a team), file conflicts read "at least N conflicts", and when none is seen it says "No conflict in the observed part" with the same line; the file count reads "at least N files", and an empty list says "No file observed (incomplete)" instead of "No files yet". A conflict that is shown stays proven; only the totals and the absences are qualified. On the canvas, the Stats box under an agent reads "at least N tools" (or "no tool observed (incomplete)") and the cost pill and the agent's row in the Cost panel read "~$X (incomplete)", never "at least" (see [Cost](cost-and-usage.md#cost-the--figures)), from that agent's own session only, like the agent card; the box grows to hold the longer text (never past 240 px: a line that would still be wider drops its duration, never its qualifier). The graph outline read by screen readers says "at least N tool calls" or "no tool call observed (incomplete)" and the same "(incomplete)" cost for the same agents. The Cost panel header (cost and tokens) and the totals the top bar reads aloud read "(incomplete)", never "at least", when any agent of the view, or any usage in it that belongs to no agent (the unattributed remainder), belongs to a session that lost events, each session counted once; a figure that is "not reported" stays so. In the Files & repeats panel, "Repeated reads and calls" reads "at least N times" for each repeat and "at least N observed" in its summary, "No repeat observed (incomplete)" when none is seen, and adds the line giving the missing count. Nothing changes when no loss is known. The cost on a session or team halo (and the same figure in its outline heading) reads "~$X (incomplete)" when one of the sessions of the agents it adds up lost events. A token or context figure is different: a context reading is absolute, and a reading the page never received (a compaction that shrank the context, for instance) could have lowered it as well as raised it, so it is never called a lower bound. The token line under the context bar of an agent and the percentage above the context ring add "(incomplete)", the token line of the outline and the context window of the agent card add "(last reading, incomplete)", from that agent's own session only; the label space reserved on the canvas is the one of the longer text. The bar itself and the ring are drawn from the same last reading (the top bar draws no number any more, #523). In the Sessions panel, the tokens of an agent row and of the branch, session and team family totals that count such a session are marked "?" instead of "≥" (spelled "last reading (incomplete)" or "incomplete" for screen readers), their costs are marked "?" too (spelled "events lost (incomplete)"), and the totals carry "(incomplete)", whose explanation names the replay loss among its causes and says the real figure may differ. The notice is left out in full screen like the rest of the chrome. A separate live region announces it when it appears, and again when leaving full screen brings it back; nothing is announced in full screen, where no notice is drawn. Dismissing it puts the focus on the Sessions button of the top bar.

### Context readings replaced in the relay replay

The relay's replay replaces a context reading of an agent by a later reading of the same agent taken at the same instant (a tool call emits up to three in a row), when nothing shown can tell them apart: the current context of each agent, the gauge at every instant of a review, the agents and their states are the same. These replaced readings are not lost events and are not counted in "at least N earlier events were not replayed"; the replay keeps more of the session for the same caps. Readings of different instants are all kept. A [session export](../session-export.md) of a replayed session has no row for a replaced reading. Rules and measures: [relay-sources.md](../relay-sources.md#replay-buffer-context-readings-replaced-at-the-same-instant-567).

### Sessions the page freed from its own memory

The page also keeps its own buffer of each session's events, to rebuild a view when you come back to it, and caps it in memory: 64 MiB for all the sessions together (and 5000 events per session, 50 sessions), plus 50 000 events or 32 MiB for the buffer of All and the team views (see [Performance](../performance.md#memory-of-the-event-buffers-659)). Past it, the sessions written least recently are freed and the oldest events of All are dropped.

- When a view lacks events for that reason, the [Graph truncated](#graph-truncated) notice says so, with the exact number: "N older events freed from the page's memory" (alone it reads "Graph incomplete: N older events freed from the page's memory"), with a line of its own under **Losses** in **Details** and in the short line of the Sessions panel header. N comes from the buffers' counters, never from an estimate: coming back to a session the page freed since you left it, the view you left is kept and continues with the events still in memory, and N is the number of events received between your departure and the moment it was freed; coming back to a session the page trimmed (older events dropped, its agent creations kept), the view starts again from the events still in memory and N is every event the trims dropped; in All or a team view, N counts only the events of the sessions that view shows, those dropped after you left it (all of them when the view is rebuilt). The number stays while you come and go, since the canvas still lacks those events, and **Reload session events** recounts it. It adds to the total the dismissal of the notice keeps, so the notice comes back when more is freed.
- A freed session that receives new events holds only those, without its earlier events, so the calls of an agent whose creation the page no longer has are not drawn. A [session export](../session-export.md) says how many earlier events were dropped only for a session still in memory (a freed one, not written since, exports no event).
