## Panels

- **Context**: the project context of a session (`CLAUDE.md`, the memory index and the issue numbers they cite). It is loaded only when the panel opens or you refresh it, and a failure is shown as a failure. Below it, with the standalone relay, a table lists the open pull requests of the session's repository by branch with the state of their checks (the PR of the session's own branch reads "(this session)"), and the open issues no agent holds. "no checks reported" is not "passing", and "At least N ... shown" means the list is partial. See [node-inspector.md](../node-inspector.md). A **CAPABILITIES** block lists the instruction files, skills and MCP servers of the session (see [Skills, plugins and memory](project-context.md#skills-plugins-and-memory)). At the top, a **RESUME** block gives the command that reopens the session from a terminal (see [Resuming a session](project-context.md#resuming-a-session)).
- **System**: the health of the machine that runs the relay (CPU, memory, swap, disk, load), opened with the top bar button, the command palette or **Y**. A metric that could not be read says "Not available" and has no meter, it is never 0; the severity (Normal, Warm from 85 %, Critical from 95 %) is in words and an icon. Local clients only, not available inside VS Code or in demo mode. See [system-stats.md](../system-stats.md).
- **Sessions, Timeline, Files, Conversation**: the panels listed in the README.
- **Conversation: choosing an agent** (owner's request, option B). With fewer than 5 agents that have messages, the panel keeps one tab per agent plus *All*: the tabs wrap onto new lines instead of scrolling sideways, all of them shown (at most five tabs, so no cap on the height and no vertical scroll that would hide one without a sign, #613), and **Filter pair** goes under them when the panel is narrow. From 5 agents with messages on, a single button replaces the tabs: **Agent: All (83)**, or the chosen agent and its state (**Agent: builder-07 (Thinking)**); its visible text is its accessible name, and a long agent name wraps onto a second line instead of being cut. An agent chosen on the canvas, in the Sessions panel or by clicking a message row is shown by the button too. The button opens, inside the panel, under the button (it pushes the message list down and never covers it), a search field and the list of the agents with messages; the open area takes at most 70 % of the panel height (or 16rem when the panel is short), the list is never less than three options and a group heading high (28 px options; under 768 px, 44 px options and 160 px, so that browsing with Up and Down keeps three whole options in view past the first two), and when the panel cannot hold that much the open area scrolls vertically as a whole; nothing scrolls sideways:
  - the search matches the agent name or its team name, case and accents ignored, and also finds finished agents; a line says how many agents are listed out of how many (*5 agents of 83*), or *No matching agent*;
  - the agents are grouped: first the agents of no team, the main agent among them (one group per session when several sessions speak, else *No team (n)*: the unfinished agents of a team or workflow are counted in their own group, so this group is not the count of unfinished agents), then one group per team or workflow (*Workflow mega (52)*), then *Finished (n)*. The finished agents are folded when there are 6 or more of them, unless the current agent is one: the **Finished (n)** button beside the search field unfolds them (its state is said by `aria-expanded` and a chevron) and keeps their count in view, so nothing disappears without a trace;
  - each option says, in words, the agent name, its state, its total number of messages and, when new messages arrived while another agent was shown, *some unread* (*5 messages, some unread*: the number is the total, how many are unread is not counted); the current choice has a check mark, read as "current choice". When agents have unread messages, a pill **n agents unread** (*1 agent unread*, *3 agents unread*) beside the button (n counts agents, not messages) opens the list on them (*Unread only*, a toggle in the list). Messages that already existed when the panel mounted are not counted as unread (before, opening the panel on one agent marked every other agent unread);
  - keyboard: the button opens with Enter, Space, Down or Up and the focus goes to the search field; Up and Down move through the options (the active one has the focus outline), Enter chooses and closes, Escape closes and gives the focus back to the button, Tab past the last control closes the list. On opening, the current agent's option is the active one and is scrolled into view, even when it was chosen elsewhere. When the 5th agent speaks while a tab has the focus, the focus moves to the button instead of being lost, and back to the active tab when the agents fall under 5; focus elsewhere is left where it is;
  - Shift-click or Shift+Enter on an option filters on the messages exchanged with the current agent (the Shift-click on a tab, kept without tabs). **Filter pair** stays on the row of the button; under 768 px the button, the options, **Filter pair** and its two lists are 44 px high, and the two lists are no longer cut at 120 px.
  The *Teams* / *Teams and workflows* block under the agent row is folded while the agent list is open (on a short panel it took the height the list needed) and comes back when it closes. Otherwise it is left as it was: it also lists the members that have no message yet and their role (main agent, subagent, teammate), which the picker, made of the agents with messages, does not; making its names clickable would add a second way to do what the picker does.
  Measured in Chromium on the demo with 83 agents (`?scenario=workflow83`): before, the row of tabs was 12 508 px wide in a 240 px visible strip at 1280 px (1 tab of 84 fully visible; 11 905 px for 280 px and 2 tabs in English), and an agent chosen from the message list stayed 12 369 px right of the strip. After, at 320 x 640, 390 x 800 and 1280 x 800, in French and in English: the agent area never scrolls sideways, all 83 agents and *All* are reachable by a vertical scroll only, and the chosen agent's option is in view on opening (the list is 163 px high at 320 x 640, 161 px at 640 x 400, the open area scrolling vertically to show it whole; 317 px at 1280 x 800). At 640 x 400, i.e. 1280 x 800 zoomed to 200 %, the list was 15 px high before, with no option visible at any scroll position. Now, browsing with Up and Down, the active option is always whole in view and three whole options are visible from the third option on (two on the first two, where the *No team* and *Workflow mega* headings share the 161 px window with *All* and the main agent). Scrolled with the wheel to an arbitrary position, the window shows one to three whole options (fewer than three at about half the positions, when headings fall inside): the panel is 230 px high at that size and its header takes 61 px of it, so three whole 44 px options at every position do not fit.
- **One name per thing** (#372, the words come from `web/lib/ui-glossary.ts` and a test fixes them):
  - **Context** is the project panel (key P: project CLAUDE.md, memory, issues). The gauge of the model's tokens, in the agent card, the legend and the guided tours, is the **Context window**, never "Context" alone.
  - **Timeline** is the panel opened by T. The slider of the control bar is the **Playback position**.
  - **Sessions and agents** is the list opened by L: the top bar button, the Shortcuts dialog and the command palette use that name.
  - **Active only** (Sessions panel) lists the sessions heard from that are still followed and not marked finished, plus the selected one. It hides at once a session whose end was observed or that is no longer watched (its transcript passed the size cap, could not be read, or left the scan scope), and one never heard from: it has no time limit, unlike **Hide inactive** of the top bar, which keeps a finished session in the All view for 10 minutes after its last event. Its state is kept in this browser with the other interface preferences (the project and runtime filters of the panel among them), so a reload keeps it. The two filters keep their own rules (a choice made on purpose, [#372](https://github.com/j0hn-42/agent-lens/issues/372)): so that a session drawn on the canvas but missing from the list (or the reverse) is explained, a line under the panel header says both settings as they are set, with no control of its own: "List: Active only" or "List: all sessions" (said only when nothing narrows the list: as soon as the search or the project, runtime or branch filter is on, it says "List: filtered", or "List: Active only, filtered"; the line states the settings, not a count), then "Canvas: Hide inactive (10 min)" in All sessions, "Canvas: Hide inactive" in one session or a team (the canvas filter then has no time window), "Canvas: Hide inactive off" when it is off (it does not claim that everything is drawn: the project scope, closed sessions and expired cards still apply), and "Canvas: Hide inactive paused during the tour" while the guided tour shows the inactive agents for a moment (the setting is still on and applies again when the tour ends, #613) (*Liste : Actives seulement* / *toutes les sessions* / *filtrée* / *Actives seulement, filtrée*, *Canevas : Masquer les inactifs (10 min)* / *Masquer les inactifs* / *Masquer les inactifs désactivé* / *Masquer les inactifs suspendu pendant la visite*). The middle dot between the two halves is drawn, not read. Measured in the demo, the line adds 22.5 px to the panel header when it holds on one line and 39 px when it wraps (at 320 px in both languages, and at 1440 px in French); nothing is cut and nothing scrolls sideways at 320 px nor at 640 x 512, in English and in French.
  - **Hide session** (the ✕ of a session row, or Delete on the row) removes the row from this page only: the session comes back on its next event or after a reconnect, and the toast offers Undo. Nothing is closed or stopped.
- **Session export**: the ⤓ button of a session row saves it as a standalone HTML replay. The file states its own limits: events dropped before the export ("Incomplete history"), the number of secrets redacted (best effort), stream times, and agent states taken only from lifecycle events (otherwise "unknown"). See [session-export.md](../session-export.md).
- **State markers** (agent rows of the Sessions panel, #381): the mark before an agent name has one shape per state, so neither its colour nor the pulse (gone under reduced motion) is the only cue: *Idle* a plain ring, *Thinking* a filled disc, *Calling tool* a larger filled square, *Waiting for permission* a larger dashed ring (the dashed ring of the canvas), *Paused* two vertical bars, *Complete* a check mark, *Error* a cross. An agent in *last known state* gets the plain ring of *Idle*. The row also says the state in words.
- **Renaming a session**: in the Sessions panel, the pencil button (or F2 on a row) gives a session a name of your own. Enter saves, Escape cancels, an empty name restores the original. The name lives only in this browser (local storage); it is never sent to the relay or written to a transcript. In the Sessions panel a renamed session keeps its original label in the tooltip and for screen readers; elsewhere (canvas, top bar) only your name is shown. If the session leaves the list while you type, the edit is dropped. The panel lists active sessions first, most recent activity first, and groups them by project.

- **The two cards of the left column** (owner's request): the agent card and **System** are read, not glanced at, so each asks for 320 x 420 px instead of 240 x 240, their rows are drawn at 12 px (the model badge and the platform note stay at 11 px) and an agent's name at 14 px. A card never takes that room at another panel's expense: with both cards open they share the column evenly (320 x 372 at 1600 x 900, 320 x 242 at 1024 x 640, both 420 px above 1900 x 1000), and when a card would leave the timeline or System no place it gives back height, then width (240 px), before anything falls to a full-width sheet. Measured over the layout's whole test space (viewports x open panels x the message list and the agent chat beside them): 1 246 layouts dock where 1 182 did before, and none that used to dock became a sheet. Below 900 px of window width both are sheets as before. Each scrolls when its content is taller than the room it got, and the card's body takes the focus for that scroll only while it really scrolls. Selecting a node no longer draws a dashed line from it towards the left: that line aimed at a fixed spot in the middle of the left edge, which the card stopped using when it moved into the column, so it ended in empty space.

### In your language

- the agent picker of the Conversation panel: the button (*Agent : Tous (83)*, *Agent : builder-07 (Réflexion)*), the pill *1 agent non lu* / *3 agents non lus*, the search field *Rechercher un agent*, *Non lus seulement*, the button and group *Terminés (30)*, the group names (*Sans équipe (1)*, *Session … (n)*, *Workflow mega (52)*, *Équipe alpha (4)*), the count line (*5 agents sur 83*), *Aucun agent ne correspond*, the key hint, and in each option the state, the number of messages, *, dont des non lus* and the current choice (*choix actuel*). Agent names are data and declare `lang="en"`; the plurals follow `Intl.PluralRules` and the counts are written for the language (*1 234* in French, *1,234* in English).
- the agent card in full (model, context window, freshness and silence, loop health, git strip, delegation report, issue links, the card of a node that left the graph) and the Sessions panel (title, search and filters, session actions and states, announcements). In the card and the panel every figure keeps its qualification ("at least" becomes "au moins", "estimated" "estimé", "not reported" "non communiqué"). The columns of the panel (their names, and the accessible wording of every cell: *modèle non communiqué*, *branche non enregistrée*, *au moins 12k estimé jetons*, *durée inconnue*, *début de la session non enregistré*, *estimation du coût du contexte … incomplète*), the roles of the agent rows (*sous-agent*, *coéquipier*) and the announcement of agents that stop reporting (*… n’émet plus, dernier état connu affiché*) follow the language too, with the same figures and the same qualifiers; durations such as `3m 12s` and the `≥` and `~` marks are figures and stay as drawn. The canvas draws an English status line under each agent, and it stays English there and in the text tree of the canvas. In French an agent row quotes that line exactly as the canvas draws it, in an element declaring `lang="en"`, then glosses it in French, as the legend does. The canvas draws that line with one function, and the row quotes the same function (a test fails if the canvas draws anything else). So a teammate at work quotes its activity and glosses that activity (*working : Au travail*, *idle : Inactif* even while its state still says thinking), and a stale agent quotes its last known state (*last known state: Waiting for permission : en attente d’autorisation depuis 7 min*), never a live word. A fresh agent quotes its state word (*thinking : Réflexion*). A row that shows a tool name shows the tool as it came and quotes no word. The canvas may shorten a long line to fit under the node; the row quotes it in full. The quote and its gloss are never cut by an ellipsis: the text wraps, and on a stale row it takes its own line under the agent name, down to 320 px. The Σ totals (*Σ ~$0.129 · 13k estimé (incomplet)*, *inconnu* when no agent reported a token count, their accessible names *total de la branche*, *total de la session*, *total de la famille* and the explanation of the incomplete badge), the relative time of the Activity column (*il y a 2 min*, *à l’instant*) and the agent counts of the panel header (*30 agents : 0 actif · 4 derniers connus · 26 terminés*) are in your language too, with the same figures and qualifiers; no passage of the panel is marked English any more except the quoted state word. Names, paths, branches, model names, git commands and what an agent returned are data and stay as they came.
- the Timeline panel (#373): its region (*Chronologie d’exécution*), the *Sequence* and *Table view* buttons (*Séquence*, *Vue en tableau*, whose name is their visible text), the scroll area, the accessible name of the canvas (*Chronologie de 3 agents, de 0:00 à 1:05*, with the plural of the language: *1 agent*, *0 agent*, *2 agents*), the columns and the states of the table (*Agent*, *État*, *Début*, *Fin*, *en cours* for a block still running; *Inactif*, *Réflexion*, *Appel d’outil*, *En attente d’autorisation*, *Erreur*, *Terminé*, the words of the legend of the panel), the table of messages between agents (*Heure*, *Type*, *De*, *Vers*, *Contenu*; *Envoi*, *Retour*, *Retour (erreur)*, *Message*), the message buttons (*orchestrator -> explore - ENVOI*), the line *+n messages antérieurs, voir la vue en tableau* (singular and plural by the rule of the language, the number written for it) and the empty state (*Aucune donnée de chronologie pour l’instant*), in the table and on the canvas. The words the canvas draws inside a block follow the language too, since the panel draws them itself: the fixed labels (*Réflexion…*, *Démarrage*, *Terminé*, *Autorisation*, *Repris*, *ÉCHEC*, *ANNULÉ*, and the suffix of a call that expired) and, in a block too narrow for its label, the letter of its state, one letter per state and distinct in each language (*I R O A E T* in French, *I T C P E D* in English). Times (`1:05`) are figures and stay as drawn. The name of the agents, the name of a tool and its arguments (`Read: src/foo.ts`) and the content of a message are data: they stay as they came, in an element declaring `lang="en"`, and so does the part of a block label that is a tool call, while its outcome word (*ÉCHEC*) is French. The pair filter chip under the timeline speaks the language too (it used to stay English there). The hidden table that reads the canvas no longer sticks out of the panel, and a long tool argument in the table view wraps instead of widening it. Measured in a browser against the demo in French, at 320 x 568 and 640 x 512, the header, the legend and the table view fit with nothing cut, covered or scrolling sideways (`tests-a11y/e2e/i18n-fr-panels-reflow.e2e.ts`); the demo has no link channel when it opens, so the *Séquence* button and the list of messages are not measured in a browser, only in jsdom.
- the titles of the panels (#557, WCAG 3.2.4): a panel has one name in a language, the same in the command palette, in the name of its top bar button and in its accessible name (the System panel also shows it as its title, the Files & repeats panel in capitals; the other headings contain it): *Fichiers et répétitions*, *Conversation*, *Contexte*, *Système*, *Coût*, *Chronologie*, *Statistiques* (*Files & repeats*, *Conversation*, *Context*, *System*, *Cost*, *Timeline*, *Stats* in English). The Cost overlay and the Stats overlay are drawn on the canvas and have no title of their own, so they keep their button and their palette name. The accessible name of a panel opened as a drawer under 768 px is that name, carried by a hidden element that declares the language (an `aria-label` cannot), so a screen reader pronounces it with the right voice. The headings Files & repeats, Timeline and Context show *FICHIERS ET RÉPÉTITIONS* (*FILES & REPEATS*, #518), *CHRONOLOGIE D’EXÉCUTION* and *CONTEXTE DU PROJET*. The French text of the Settings reset help names the panels the same way. The content of the Conversation, Files & repeats, Context and Timeline panels is translated (see the list of surfaces below); the Cost and Stats overlays are drawn by the canvas, in English, except the *Cost totals* text region, which is read in the interface language (see **Top bar layout** in [Top bar](top-bar.md)).
- the content of the Conversation, Context and Files & repeats panels (#599, suite de #373): in **Conversation**, the pill and its accessible name, the header (*Appels d’outils*, *Exporter la conversation*, search), the agent tabs (*Tous*, *terminé*, unread), the pair filter (*Filtrer une paire*, its chip, its announcement and its empty state), the team chips, the role of each row (*RÉFLEXION*, *UTILISATEUR*, *ENVOI*, *RETOUR*, *RETOUR (ERREUR)*, *COMPACTÉ*), *Copier*, *Voir tout (+n caractères)*, the marker of dropped messages, the count of new messages, *Aller au dernier message*, the copy and export feedback and the words around each tool call (the edit summaries, *Bloc 1 sur 2*, *Avancement des tâches*, *lignes 5–14*, the result summary); in **Files & repeats**, the empty and incomplete states, the conflicts and who edited what, the tokens of each file and of the total, the steps of a file (*3 étapes observées (incomplet)*), *Lectures et appels répétés* and its summary (*Lectures et appels répétés : au moins 5 cibles répétées observées*, #518) and *Actualiser*; in **Context**, the project files and their states (*introuvable pour ce projet*, *Tronqué : les 64 premiers Ko sur 97 Ko*), the issues, *REPRISE* and *Aller au terminal*, *CAPACITÉS* (instructions, skills, MCP servers, with their *au moins* lower bounds) and the pull requests and issues of the session (*vérifications inconnues*, *il peut y en avoir d’autres*). Each panel declares its language with `lang`. What the data brings is shown as it came and declares `lang="en"`: file names and paths, the text of messages, thinking and tool calls, tool output, the content of CLAUDE.md and of the memory file, the resume command, branch names and the titles of pull requests and issues. A figure keeps its qualification: *au moins*, *estimé*, *incomplet*, *non communiqué*, and a count over a graph that lost events stays a lower bound.
- the line under the Timeline canvas (#659): *n blocs plus anciens non affichés* (*n older blocks not shown*, singular and plural by the rule of the language, the number written for it). The Timeline keeps at most 2 000 blocks per agent; when a long session goes past it, the oldest 10 % leave in one batch and this line counts every block that left, summed over the agents, so nothing disappears silently. The line is absent while no block was dropped. The canvas draws only the rows in view (plus two on each side) and keeps its full height, so the scroll bar is unchanged. Clearing the history resets the count.
