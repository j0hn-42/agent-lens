## Top bar

- **Session scope** (top bar, standalone app and dev relay): this workspace, one project, or all workspaces. The selector is in **More** (see the layout below); while More is closed, More carries a mark and reads the sentence and the discovery ceilings in its description. The ceilings are announced by a polite live region of the bar, there from the first render and empty while there is nothing to say (a region added with its text already in it is not announced by every screen reader); the selector in More writes the same text without announcing it a second time. The sentence next to the selector is what the relay reports it scans ("Scanning this workspace only (name)"; nothing is written on all workspaces), not what you asked: the selector only moves once the relay confirmed. On a single workspace an empty Sessions and agents list says that the scope is limited and how to widen it. A discovery ceiling that was hit (more than 500 transcripts in a folder, a transcript too large, the watch limit) is written next to the selector as "at least N": it is a partial view, not an absence. Choosing a project narrows the Sessions and agents list and the All view to it and leaves the relay scope as it is. Changing the scope reloads the view (a notice says so). See [relay-sources.md](../relay-sources.md).
- **Top bar layout** (owner request of 2026-10-21, "the top bar must stay on one line"; [#523](https://github.com/j0hn-42/agent-lens/issues/523) before it): the bar is one line at every width, in English and in French, also in the worst case: a 40-character session name, the scope with its three discovery ceilings, an attention counter with errors, DEMO with **Exit demo** or OFFLINE with **Retry now** and its reconnect detail, blocked Notifications, a filter on and both quota gauges. It is 38.5 px high from 768 px and 56 px under 768 px (44 px targets), measured in Chromium at 1920, 1440, 1280, 1100, 1024, 768, 390 and 320 px by `tests-a11y/e2e/topbar-layout.e2e.ts`; nothing wraps, nothing leaves the window and nothing scrolls sideways. What does not fit is behind **More**, at fixed widths (the bar never measures itself, so nothing jumps back and forth):
  - from 1280 px: the Sessions button, the connection state (with **Retry now** or **Exit demo**), the attention counter, then on the right the seven panel buttons, **Commands** and **More**;
  - 1024 to 1279 px: the same without Commands and the connection buttons, which are in More;
  - 768 to 1023 px: Sessions, the connection state, the attention counter, Commands and More; the panel buttons are in More and the panels stay docked;
  - under 768 px: Sessions (the selection, ending in an ellipsis when the line is full; the number of sessions is read, not drawn; the ⚠ of a known loss and the dot of new activity are always drawn), the connection state (its word, without the dot), the attention counter, and More as an icon (with the number of filters on); a panel opens as a drawer.

  The Sessions button draws the selection ("All sessions", a session name) and reads "Sessions: " before it, so its accessible name and its tooltip start with "Sessions: " and give the whole name. At least 10 characters of the selection are drawn at every width above, in both languages, worst case included (measured by the same test). Under 1440 px the attention counter is a number ("! 3"): its name starts with that number, then gives the full text, and its tooltip too; spelled out, it left no room for the selection at 768 and 1280 px. **Notifications blocked** is in More: More then carries the warning triangle and its description says "Notifications blocked".

  **More** is a disclosure button that opens an overlay under the bar, over the canvas: it never adds a row and the bar keeps its height. It always holds the session scope, the quota gauges and the tools (sound, full screen, Settings, keyboard shortcuts), and also what its width moved there. The view filters (Notifications, **Hide inactive**) are buttons of the bar while it has room for them (the Sessions button keeps at least 240 px); when the bar is too narrow, or the Sessions button too squeezed, they move behind More, which then counts the active ones and signals blocked notifications. Under 768 px they are always behind More. Enter or Space opens it, Tab goes from More into it (its content comes right after it in the DOM, so the Tab order is the order on screen), Escape or a click outside closes it, and Escape gives the focus back to More without closing a panel. A panel, a dialog or full screen opened from More closes it, and the focus comes back to More; a filter keeps it open, so several can be changed in a row. What More holds is never hidden without a sign: with a filter on it reads "More · 1 filter" (its name lists the filters, see [Filters](filters.md#hide-inactive-and-what-it-hides)); when it holds something the bar does not show it carries a mark, a warning triangle when one of these needs a look (a discovery ceiling, a failed change of scope, a runtime problem, a low, critical or unknown quota, the link down and its reconnect detail, notifications blocked by the browser) and a dot otherwise (the sentence of the scope, the quota figures). Its description reads them in full ("Scope: Scanning this workspace only (alpha) …. Quota: 5 h 38 % left, 7 d 8 % left critical."). A resize or a browser zoom that moves the focused control behind More opens More and keeps the focus on that control; one that brings it back to the bar keeps the focus as well. The Sessions button is at most 320 px wide and gives way first when the line is full: the selection then ends in an ellipsis, and its tooltip and its accessible name give the whole text (the label, the number of sessions, the sessions not observed, which it draws short from 1280 px, "2 not observed"; the Sessions panel marks each of them). The seven panel buttons (Files & repeats, Conversation, Context, System, Cost, Timeline, Stats) are icons at every width; Commands and Shortcuts keep their text. An icon keeps the name of the button for screen readers and voice control ("Files & repeats", "Conversation (C)", "Context (P)", "System (Y)", "Cost", "Timeline", "Stats"; in French "Fichiers et répétitions", "Conversation (C)", "Contexte (P)", "Système (Y)", "Coût", "Chronologie", "Statistiques"). Hover or focus any panel button to read its tooltip: its name and shortcut, and for Context, System and Cost also what the panel shows (the Cost one gives the method of the cost estimate). Screen readers read that last kind after the name; a tooltip that only repeats the name and the shortcut is not read a second time. The tooltip stays while the pointer is on it, Escape hides it without moving the focus, and a click on the button hides it until the pointer comes back, so it never covers the panel that opens. An active panel button is underlined by a bar, not only coloured. The bar draws no summary text any more ([#523](https://github.com/j0hn-42/agent-lens/issues/523)): no tokens in context, no cost and no agent counts. The Cost panel draws the totals of the view, the Sessions panel those of each session, the inspector those of each agent. The agent counts of the view (`30 agents: 0 active · 4 last known · 26 done`, or `3 sessions · 12 agents` in All) are drawn in the header of the **Sessions** panel, with the same words; screen readers hear them in full ("4 last known state") there, and also in the bar, followed by the cost and the tokens in context of the view. The Cost panel is drawn on the canvas, which is not read, so while it is open it also carries its totals as text in a region named "Cost totals" (*Totaux du coût*), read in the interface language with the same figures and qualifiers as the drawing and the bar (*at least*, *estimated*, *not reported*; *au moins*, *estimé*, *non communiqué*). It follows the drawing: it gives the cost and the tokens in context of the header, then, in every view (one session or All), the unattributed row with its tokens and its cost read "about" for the drawn "~" (priced at the default rate, the model being unknown), and "estimated" when the drawing says so ("including 900k unattributed tokens (about $5.40 estimated)"; *dont 900k jetons non attribués (environ $5.40 estimé)*). When the panel draws nothing (no agent with a figure, nothing unattributed), the region is not there either, so it never reads "$0.00". It stays in full screen, where the canvas still draws the panel while the bar is hidden. The bar keeps its text, with the same wording: the Cost panel exists only while open, and the totals must stay readable without opening a panel ([#555](https://github.com/j0hn-42/agent-lens/issues/555)). Its unattributed part has the same wording, qualifiers and order as the Cost region above ([#576](https://github.com/j0hn-42/agent-lens/issues/576)): cost, tokens, then the unattributed part, in every view, and nothing when there are no unattributed tokens. It is no longer a bare "including $X unattributed", and it no longer depends on the cost being reported, as the drawing does not. The per-agent and BY TOOL rows of the Cost panel still have no text equivalent, on purpose: each agent row carries its own loss qualifier and its name is masked in presentation mode, and the same figures per agent are already read in the inspector, the graph outline and the Sessions panel, so a second list here would duplicate them without a proof of its own.
- **Quota gauges (top bar)**: in **More**, two small gauges tell what is left of the Claude subscription quota: `5 h [bar] 38 % left` and `7 d [bar] 66 % left`. Hover or focus a gauge to read when it resets; Escape hides the tooltip. While More is closed it carries a dot (a warning triangle when a gauge is low, critical or unknown) and reads the figures in its description. What they mean, when they say `quota: unknown for now` and how to turn them on or off is in [Subscription quota gauges](cost-and-usage.md#subscription-quota-gauges).
- **Agents that need you**: the "! 2 agents waiting / 1 agent in error" button of the top bar (the "!" is decorative, screen readers read the words; in French "2 agents en attente / 1 agent en erreur") counts the agents waiting for a permission or in error. Each click selects the next one after the selected agent, waiting agents first, then wraps around; an agent of another session switches to that session instead. With a single agent it selects that one and does not promise a "next". Outside VS Code its tooltip adds that a permission is answered where the agent runs (its terminal or editor): the page draws no permission card there.
- **Folder prefix of session tabs**: the folder prefix appears when the sessions you have open come from two or more folders, a tab reads "folder / name" (the Sessions button in the top bar, and the sessions of a team in the Sessions panel) so two sessions called "fix login" can be told apart. The folder is the repository's folder name, proven by the git common dir (worktrees of one repository share it). Limits: a session outside git, or one under "Imported transcripts", gets no prefix, because no folder is proven; with a single folder nothing is added; under a project heading of the Sessions panel the prefix is not repeated, the heading already names the folder; a name that already starts with the folder is left as is. The prefix is display only: it is never written into the name you chose, and the canvas halos keep showing the session name and workspace as before.

### In your language

- the whole top bar (#373, #500, #501): the bar declares the interface language with `lang`, and so do its translated passages. Buttons and tooltips: *Hide inactive* (*Masquer les inactifs*, worded as in Settings, its accessible name and tooltip too), Notifications, mute, full screen, Commands, Settings and Keyboard shortcuts, and the seven panel buttons (*Fichiers et répétitions*, *Conversation*, *Contexte*, *Système*, *Coût*, *Chronologie*, *Statistiques*; the Context tooltip in VS Code is translated too). The Sessions button ("Sessions : Toutes les sessions", its tooltip, "n sessions non observées", the hidden "nouvelle activité dans une autre session" and "graphe incomplet, pertes listées dans le panneau Sessions" read after the ⚠ of a loss), the *More* button (*Plus*), its name with the active filters and its description (*Connexion :*, *Portée :*, *Quota :*; the whole name is in your language), the *View controls* and *Side panels* groups and, in More, the *Filtres de la vue* and *Outils* groups, the *Scope* selector (its name, its tooltip, its options, the sentence that says what is scanned and the discovery ceilings, which keep their "at least" as *au moins*), the connection state (*EN DIRECT*, *CONNECTÉ*, *CONNEXION*, *HORS LIGNE*, *DÉMO*, with the reconnect detail and the *Retry now* and *Exit demo* buttons) and the totals read by screen readers (agent counts, tokens, cost, the part that belongs to no agent) are in your language too, with the same figures and the same qualification (*au moins*, *estimé*, *non communiqué*). The attention counter is spelled out instead of the former "2 waiting / 1 error" from 1440 px: "2 agents waiting / 1 agent in error" (*2 agents en attente / 1 agent en erreur*), with an accessible name that starts with that text, then says what a click does, and a tooltip; under 1440 px it draws the number of agents and its name starts with that number, then gives the same text. What the relay or the link wrote in English (a runtime problem such as "OpenCode: database not found.", a failed change of scope, a reconnect detail the interface does not know) is shown as it came and declares `lang="en"`. The playback bar words the connection state in your language too (*EN DIRECT*, *HORS LIGNE*, see the playback bar in [Review and playback](playback.md#in-your-language)); the panel titles follow the language (see the panel titles in [Panels](panels.md#in-your-language)) and the words drawn by the canvas stay English. In French the bar holds on one line at every width too, exactly as high as in English (38.5 px from 768 px, 56 px under 768 px), with the worst case above, measured by `tests-a11y/e2e/topbar-layout.e2e.ts`. At 320 px nothing leaves the window or scrolls sideways. When the line is full the selection drawn on the Sessions button ends in an ellipsis, in either language: its tooltip and its accessible name give the whole text, from "Sessions : ", and at least 10 characters of the selection stay drawn at every width measured. This replaces the two rows from 1024 px and the Sessions label wrapping onto a second line (#595): the owner asked for one line.
